This private development candidate redistributes the unmodified official PyPA installer 1.1.0 wheel, licensed under MIT. Its full upstream license remains inside installer-1.1.0.dist-info/licenses/LICENSE. See packaging/bootstrap/manifest.json for official provenance and exact wheel/license hashes. It has no declared dependencies or vendored packages.

The wheel is included only in the installer bundle and source distribution. It executes temporarily from verified private staging; neither installer nor pip is installed into the RackMarshal runtime. No public release is authorized by this development candidate.

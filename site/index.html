RackMarshal™

Local-first infrastructure operations and incident management for self-hosted systems.

RackMarshal is an infrastructure operations platform designed to observe self-hosted systems, maintain durable operational state, detect and track incidents, and verify recovery.

The project grew from a working homelab operations system and is being prepared for broader use across homelabs, self-hosted infrastructure, edge systems, and small infrastructure environments.

Design principles

  • Local-first operation
  • Durable operational history
  • Evidence-based incident detection and recovery
  • Configuration-driven monitoring
  • Reproducible installation and migration
  • Minimal dependence on external services

Project status

RackMarshal is currently under active development. Initial development centers on Linux and Proxmox environments, including virtualization, storage, backups, hardware health, and service availability.
